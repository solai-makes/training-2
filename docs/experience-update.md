# Solaistore experience update

- Shop and staff headers have a Dark/Light switch. The choice is saved on this browser and shared between tabs on the same origin. Each port stores its own preference.
- Shopping filters include category, availability, minimum/maximum JPY price, offers only, newest arrivals and biggest discount. Results display 24 products per page.
- Edit an existing product or add one in Staff → Inventory. Set the regular price and percentage, then enable **Schedule this offer** and choose both local dates. The server applies the offer from the start time up to, but excluding, the end time. Outside the window the regular price applies. Saved order quotes and receipts retain the original price.
- Customer → Your orders → Track order opens a separate delivery page with a four-step connected timeline. Demo milestones advance automatically from the saved confirmation timestamp: Shipped after 1 hour, Out for delivery after 5 hours, Delivered after 24 hours. This is simulated fulfillment, not a courier integration. Existing manual progress is preserved; cancelled or unconfirmed orders do not progress. Staff Orders shows the same timeline without manual delivery buttons. No additional stock movement or cash collection is generated by these milestones.
- **solai26** gives 10% off and **santhoshhere341** gives 8% off. One coupon applies per order. The JPY subtotal after product offers must be strictly greater than ¥20,000. Tax and delivery do not count towards the minimum. Coupon discount rounds to whole yen and is applied before 10% tax. The minimum remains in JPY when the payment currency is INR.
- Apply a code in Cart or Checkout. A centered animated modal covers approximately 65% of the screen and displays the coupon savings. It can be closed with its button or Escape, and closes automatically after 12 seconds. Reducing the subtotal to ¥20,000 or less removes the coupon. Checkout validates the code and current prices again. The saved receipt and PDF show the discount and code.
- `node apps/private-api/src/expand-experience.ts` adds 1,040 sample catalog products once. Existing products, orders, stock and prices are preserved. Running again does not restore deleted sample products or add duplicates. Catalog seed stock is recorded as an inventory event; historical sales are not generated.

Start the four applications inside the Dev Container using `bash start-solaistore.sh`. Shop: http://localhost:5173 . Staff: http://localhost:5174 .


The redesigned product editor has separate details, pricing and schedule sections with a fixed Save/Cancel footer. Scheduled product cards and details show a second-by-second starts-in or ends-in countdown and the exact local date. Catalog prices refresh at the schedule boundary and are checked again at checkout.

The home banner rotates through four collections every six seconds. Dots select a slide; Pause stops rotation. Hover, keyboard focus and hidden tabs pause automatic movement, and reduced-motion preferences disable automatic rotation by default.
